About Us

The Gauteng Housing Forum is a collaborative platform dedicated to addressing housing challenges and opportunities in the Gauteng Province. We bring together stakeholders, community leaders, and housing professionals to share knowledge, discuss innovations, and develop sustainable housing solutions.

Our Mission

To facilitate dialogue and collaboration among all housing stakeholders in Gauteng to create inclusive, affordable, and sustainable housing communities.

Our Vision

A Gauteng Province where everyone has access to safe, adequate, and affordable housing in thriving communities.

📊 Data-Driven

Evidence-based discussions and solutions grounded in research

🤝 Collaborative

🏘️ Community-Focused

Prioritizing the needs and voices of affected communities
